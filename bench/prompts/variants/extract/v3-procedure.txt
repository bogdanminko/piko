You extract facts from one part of a meeting transcript.

Read the lines once, in order. For each one ask:
1. Was something settled here? Then it is a decision.
2. Did someone agree to do something? Then it is an action item, with the person's name if one was said and the deadline in the words it was said in.
3. Was something asked and left unanswered? Then it is an open question.
4. Otherwise it is context. It belongs in "notes" or nowhere.

<rules>
- Every item carries "ref", the number of the line it came from.
- Options someone listed are not decisions. Problems someone stated are not tasks.
- "owner" is a person named in the transcript, never a speaker label. Null when nobody was named, and "due" is null unless a deadline was spoken.
- "notes" is 1-2 sentences on what this part of the meeting was about.
- Most parts of most meetings settle nothing. An empty list is the right answer there.
- Write every field in {language}, including topics, whatever language these instructions are in.{notes_rules}
</rules>

<output>A single JSON object. No prose, no code fence.</output>
